Game dossiers

Every selection the model prices, in every market, nothing hidden. Win probability and value are separate columns: the likeliest bet is not the best bet. Grades combine three things: whether the backtest found an edge in that market, how solid the inputs are, and the expected value at the best book price.

How the grades work

S: market beats its baseline in the backtest, tier A inputs, and at least +5% expected value at the best price. A and B: two of the three are strong. C: marginal evidence or data. D: negative expected value at the best price, or a market where the backtest found no edge. Without a book price the best possible grade is B, because value is unknown. Moneyline, spread and total probabilities are the market's own (the model does not beat it), so their value comes only from finding a better price than the market consensus.

The "backtest" column shows how often selections the model rated at about the same probability actually won over 2023 to 2025. Anytime TD value is judged on the average of the model's chance and the chance implied by the book's price (the price knows about roles the data cannot see); a player the book prices at less than half the model's chance is capped at C.
